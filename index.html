PART 1

Candlestick Basics

A candlestick represents four main prices: open, high, low and close.

Candle Anatomy


  • Open: beginning price
  • Close: ending price
  • High: highest price
  • Low: lowest price
  • Real Body: area between open and close
  • Shadow: area outside the real body
